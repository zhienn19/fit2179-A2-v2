Dengue Fever

in Malaysia

As a tropical country, Malaysia has always been hospitable to mosquito populations. The combination of high humidity, frequent rainfall, and rapid urbanisation creates the ideal breeding ground for the mosquitoes, turning a seasonal nuisance into an ongoing public health priority. Thus, locals should take preventive measures to reduce mosquito breeding.

I. Dengue Outbreak Trends

10-Year Observations on Reported Confirmed Cases

Zulkifli, Z. (2020). Dengue Statistics in Malaysia, per state in 1999 to 2019.

From the observation, the number of cases increased across 2009 to 2019. This suggests that dengue has become an increasingly significant public health challenge in Malaysia, potentially driven by rapid urbanisation, population growth,and favourable environmental conditions for mosquito breeding.

Reported Confirmed Deaths from Dengue Fever

(Khaw et al., 2024).Ten-year mortality study of dengue in Malaysia. IJID Regions.

Reported confirmed deaths peaked at the year 2015. Although mortality remains relatively low compared to total infections, the spike highlights the severe consequences of delayed diagnosis, inadequate treatment, and high transmission periods.

II. Regional Hotspots & Breakdown

Top States with Highest Number of Cases

Zulkifli, Z. (2020). Dengue Statistics in Malaysia, per state in 1999 to 2019.

Public health records show that Selangor, Kelantan, Perak, Putrajaya, and Johor had the most confirmed dengue cases over a 10-year period. It was also noted that different states ranked differently each year based on the reported confirmed cases of dengue fever. Selangor had the most Dengue cases in 2019.

III. Environmental Impacts: Rainfall & Temperature

Does Rainfall affects Mosquitoes?

Portal Data Terbuka Malaysia. (n.d.). Archive.data.gov.my.

Rainfall is typically followed by a spike in dengue cases after a short delay. However, if the rainfall is exceptionally heavy(≥200mm) it can actually have the opposite effect. This flushes away mosquito eggs and larvae while destroying stagnant water breeding sites, temporarily reducing the mosquito population.

How does Temperature Affects Mosquitoes?

Portal Data Terbuka Malaysia. (n.d.). Archive.data.gov.my.

Temperature plays an important role in mosquito survival and viral replication. Warmer conditions generally accelerate mosquito development and shorten the virus incubation period, contributing to increased dengue transmission when temperatures remain within a suitable range.

IV. Demographics & Clinical Profiles

Age distribution of Dengue Cases

Portal Data Terbuka Malaysia. (n.d.). Archive.data.gov.my.

Dengue cases were most prevalent among individuals aged 20-39 years. The nation's most socially and economically active demographic. Their daily routines—commuting to work, gathering in urban centers, and navigating outdoor spaces during peak mosquito biting hours—make them the primary targets for transmission and the main drivers of regional outbreak velocity.

Most Common Dengue Fever's Symptoms

(Ahmad et al., 2018).The Sensitivity, Specificity and Accuracy of Warning Signs in Predicting Severe Dengue, the Severe Dengue Prevalence and Its Associated Factors.

Persistent vomitting, abdominal pain and lethargy are the top 3 most common symptoms found in Dengue Patients. identifying these prevalent markers early allows healthcare providers to intervene before critical complications like platelet drops or fluid accumulation develop. Patients are advised to go to hospital if symptoms persists.

Mortality Rate Based on Age Group: Which category are at a higher risk?

(Khaw et al., 2024).Ten-year mortality study of dengue in Malaysia. IJID Regions.

While the elderly are traditionally viewed as the most vulnerable, mortality rates actually peak drastically in the 45-59 age group. This indicates that middle-aged adults face the highest fatal risk during an Outbreaks

—likely due to a combination of comorbidities and delayed hospital admission—making early clinical testing vital for this specific workforce demographic.
